# IBD Live Summary — 2026-07-24

*Source: IBD Live transcript_2026-07-24_11.10.47.txt*

## 1. Market Pulse

**Thursday, July 24 — IBD reduced market exposure from 40-60% down to 20-40%.** Persistent index damage and failed breakouts forced the downgrade. The market was deeply bifurcated: hot tech stocks faced severe pressure while healthy rotation occurred into banks, biotechs, medical, transports, and energy. Major indices (S&P, Nasdaq, Russell, RSP) dipped below key moving averages or undercut recent lows (June 9, July 8 lows). Nasdaq hovering near $25,000.

**Guest George Tkaczuk** (U.S. Investing Champion, RCM Wealth Advisors) shared his approach: he scaled up to fully invested early in the week following a Friday bounce in semiconductors/memory, but quickly trimmed 40% intraday. "You have to be in there early, or something's gonna happen over the weekend. Gaps down after 2-3 weeks of work — it just gets wiped out in one day."

**Panel:** Ali Coram (host), Justin Nielsen, David "Hatman" Saito Chung, George Tkaczuk (guest).

Justin Nielsen crystallized the moment: "Rather than try and bend down and pick up pennies and nickels in front of a steamroller, we're staying on the side and waiting until those dollars are a little bit more easy to come by."

## 6. Trading Education

**George Tkaczuk — Rapid Position Reduction**: "You have to be in there early, or something's gonna happen over the weekend. Gaps down after 2-3 weeks of work — it just gets wiped out in one day." When market indices break down, cut aggressively (40% intraday). Wait for right-side base structures before re-entering.

**Justin Nielsen — The Steamroller**: "Rather than try and bend down and pick up pennies and nickels in front of a steamroller, we're staying on the side and waiting until dollars are a little bit more easy to come by." In corrections, the best trade is often no trade.

**Dave Saito Chung — Broken Chart Hierarchy**: "Stocks severely damaged below the 50-day that approach but don't touch the 200-day or 40-week line — those might be your better candidates. Some will crush through both. The ones that hold the 200-day have a better chance of recovery." Use the 200-day as a filter for which broken charts are worth tracking.
