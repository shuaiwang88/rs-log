## 6. Trading Education & Guest Advice
- **Mark Minervini on Volatility:** Do not bottom-fish extended losers. If playing oversold bounces, wait for confirmation of an uptrend and buy stocks breaking out of bases. 
- **Mark Minervini on Risk Management:** What takes you to heaven takes you to ****. Tight stops prevent death by a thousand cuts but require precise timing. Wide stops survive shakeouts but can lead to devastating losses if the trend reverses. Sell a portion of a winner (e.g. 20%) to finance your stop going into earnings.
- **Mark Minervini on Patience:** Let the learning curve happen. Don't constantly switch strategies when things aren't working; instead, prepare for when the cycle turns back in your favor.
