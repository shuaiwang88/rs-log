## 1. Market Pulse

**Monday, July 6 — Gap-up Monday = exit opportunity.** The market opened sharply higher after a weekend of positive news flow, but Mike Webster delivered a stark warning: "Take this gift to reduce." The NASDAQ had broken below its 50-day and 21-day moving averages going into the weekend, and Webster saw the gap-up as an opportunity to trim positions, not add to them.

**Webster's logic:** "When the NASDAQ breaks the 50-day and 21-day into a weekend, and then gaps up Monday, that gap is a gift. Use it to reduce exposure. The trend is broken — rallies within a downtrend are for selling, not buying."

**Panel:** Ken Shreve, Mike Webster, Ed Carson.

**Market character:** Gap-up failed to hold as the session progressed. By mid-morning, early gains were being eroded. The pattern of gap-up-then-fade was becoming a hallmark of the correction.
