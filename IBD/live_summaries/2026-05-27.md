## 6. Trading Education

### Mike Webster — 1990s Playbook & Power Trend Management

**The 2.5-Day Down Rule:** "In bull markets, 2.5 days down shakes out weak hands and lets air out of the balloon before resuming." The recent 3-day pullback was textbook — buy the bounce.

**Historical Model Books:** Study Qualcomm, Taser, JDSU/VIAV, Schwab from the 1990s. "This is the 1990s all over again — a very special environment. But it didn't go straight up, and you can't just plant your flag."

**Portfolio ATR Management:** Sum the ATRs of all your holdings. When the total exceeds 4, dial back risk or raise cash. "This is particularly important for risk-averse accounts heading into retirement."

**Climax Action Rules:** "MU could go to $1,000+. You HAVE to have strict trailing stops. The 21-day, 10-week, Quicksand, and Grateful Dead rules exist because these reversals are brutal when they come."

### Steve Swetz — Wyckoff "Ease of Movement"

**Price/Volume Over Indicators:** "I'm immature, unorganized, lazy, and loud, but I'm fun. Notice how the ease of movement is starting to slow down some. Just look at a simple price and volume chart — you will see so much of these little details that we might miss looking at Bollinger Bands and MACD." How quickly a market recovers losses to make new highs tells you everything about the character of the trend.

### Hatman Dave — Long-Term Institutional Valuation

On NVDA: "2030 EPS estimates raised to $17.29, implying ~12x future earnings at current prices." Institutional money is pricing in 5-year growth trajectories, not this quarter's numbers.
