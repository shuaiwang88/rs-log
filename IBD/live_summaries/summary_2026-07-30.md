## 4. Macro & News Brief
A massive market catalyst was revealed: Leopold Aschenbrenner's $24 billion hedge fund ("Situational Awareness") was forced to liquidate its entire public book due to severe margin calls. The fund was heavily levered (4x) into AI longs (Nebius, CoreWeave, Micron, SanDisk, AMD) and software shorts (Adobe). This forced liquidation helps explain the brutal, rapid sell-off in AI/chips over the past week and the subsequent violent short-covering bounce seen today. Additionally, interest rates remain higher for longer (around 4.5% - 4.7%), but the market seems to be absorbing the cost of capital reasonably well.

## 6. Trading Education & Guest Advice
*   **Mike Webster on Observation vs. Trading**: In a choppy market, you don't need to put on a million trades. Keep exposure light (e.g., 1% or test buys). Build a watchlist of broken setups to see if they hold or roll over. Specifically, Mike advises making a watchlist of the liquidated hedge fund's holdings to monitor which ones naturally bounce back versus which stay broken—this will be a "great tell" for underlying strength.
*   **Hatman on Covered Calls**: Avoid writing covered calls on thinner, highly convicted stocks (like CRS) just to earn "rent." Doing so can painfully cap your upside if the stock makes an unexpected massive run. If you must use covered calls, stick to highly liquid mega-caps (like TSLA or NVDA) and keep durations very short-term while the stock builds a base.
*   **Arnie on Patience & FOMO**: The market is exceptional at drawing you in with FOMO on big green days. Don't fall for it. Demand that stocks prove themselves by reclaiming and holding above the 21-day and 50-day moving averages before committing real capital.
