## 5. Trading Education — Ascending Base Rules (Webster)

Webster delivered a detailed lesson on the three key ascending base rules:

1. **Proper spacing between highs:** Each successive high in an ascending base must have proper spacing from the prior high. "If the third high is too close to the second, the pattern is invalid."
2. **RS line must hold:** "The RS line breaking during an ascending base is a major red flag. It tells you money is leaving the stock even as it consolidates."
3. **Volume on breakouts:** "Low-volume breakouts from ascending bases fail at much higher rates. You need institutional confirmation."
4. **Internal volatility:** "Too much intra-pattern volatility suggests distribution, not accumulation. The base should tighten, not widen."
