### 6. Trading Education & Process
- **A "Bullish" Pullback After an Explosive Run:**
    Ken described AEHR's 4% decline as a "bullish 4% decline." After an explosive two-week rally, a small single-day pullback on the daily chart is merely a blip. If you're long a stock with such strong momentum, you don't overreact to a modest, normal pullback. Context matters: a 4% drop after a 60% move is not a sell signal, but a normal digestion.

- **The "Mixed Bag" in a Recovering Sector:**
    In the chip equipment group, there's a stark divergence between leaders and laggards. ASML and TER are above the 50-day line and forming constructive bases, while AMAT, LRCX, and KLAC are stuck below or at the 50-day line, facing resistance. This teaches that even when a sector is coming back, you must be highly selective. Do not buy the laggard just because it "should" catch up; the market is telling you which stocks have the strongest accumulation.

- **Accumulation/Distribution Quality Matters:**
    Ken pointed out a subtle but important concern with ASML and other recovering chip stocks: they are rallying on lighter volume, so their Accumulation Distribution ratings are not improving materially. This doesn't negate the positive price action, but it's a flag that the institutional conviction is not yet as strong as it could be. A true leader's rally should be accompanied by signs of accumulation (heavier volume up days).

- **Educating Yourself on Speculative IPOs:**
    For APMD, a small, speculative biotech IPO, Ken advised going directly to the company's website (apneamed.com) to read white papers, understand the management pedigree, and learn about the intellectual property. For early-stage companies, doing your own fundamental homework is critical before taking a position.

- **The Blue-Chip Base (FedEx):**
    FedEx is a good example of a "boring" but constructive setup. It's a well-known, widely held stock that is executing on a cost-cutting plan, which is showing up in improving earnings and revenue. It's building a proper cup base near highs. These types of liquid, slower-moving names are a great way to participate in a broad uptrend without the extreme volatility of AI names.
