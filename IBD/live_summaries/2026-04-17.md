## 3. Scott St. Clair — AAOI Trade Walkthrough (Masterclass)

Scott walked through his entire AAOI trade — a masterclass in position management during a power trend:

1. **Entry Day:** Bought at ~$42 when market opened down 5% but closed strong. "The market had a chance to go down and they couldn't take it down. That's the day I'm buying AAOI."
2. **Accumulation:** "I might buy it 3-4 times that day. Closes near the high, I'll buy more on the close."
3. **Adding:** "Next day closes on the high, I'll buy more. Next day breaking out — now I can buy again because I have a big cushion."
4. **First Trim:** "Next day opens higher — I love to sell gap opens. 20-25-30% of position."
5. **Big Trim:** "The raw numbers start to affect me. Everything else is working. Probably half gone if not more."
6. **Final Position:** "Probably had 20% of position when it came down."
7. **Re-Add & Exit:** "Yesterday's bar was really good. I gunned it again with the idea to sell on the open. My whole objective was to sell it today. I don't own a single share anymore."
8. **The Risk:** "The trade is done — risk is I get left in the dust like LITE. Sold at 200, now it's 800. Ugh."
9. **Round Lots:** "If I have 1,000 shares, I'll sell 300. I don't like having 182 shares — I'll go buy 118 to make it 300."
