## 1. Market Pulse

**Thursday, July 16 — Market "in repair."** The market was showing tentative signs of stabilization after the correction that began in early July. NASDAQ trying to reclaim its 50-day moving average but facing resistance. S&P 500 showing better relative strength with defensive rotation supporting. Dow Jones hovering near highs on financials and healthcare strength.

**Key catalysts:** TSMC announced an additional **$100 billion US investment** in advanced chip manufacturing — the second major US expansion. UNH reported a stunning **+56% EPS gain** with strong guidance raise. These were the two pillars of the day's narrative.

**Panel:** Ken Shreve (host), Arnie Gutierrez, Justin Nielsen, Ed Carson.

**Core theme:** The market is "in repair" — not broken enough to be fully bearish, not strong enough to be bullish. Panelists advocating patience, small positions, and waiting for a follow-through day. Semis showing signs of life but breadth still narrow.
