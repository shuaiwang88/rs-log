## 6. Trading Education — Fitzpatrick

**The Win Rate Rule:** "When your win rate drops, that's the market telling you to pull back. Don't argue with it. Reduce size, raise cash, wait. The market will present opportunities again — it always does."

**Rent vs. Long-Term:** "In this environment, treat positions as rentals, not long-term holdings. If it works for a few days, great — take the gain. If it doesn't work immediately, cut it. Don't get married to any position in a correction."

**Market Timing:** "The single best indicator of whether a breakout will work is the market itself. In a correction, most breakouts fail. Wait for the follow-through day."
