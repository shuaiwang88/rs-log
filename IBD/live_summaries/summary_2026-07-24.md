## 6. Trading Education & Guest Advice
- **George Tkaczuk on Round Numbers:** Watch massive psychological valuation levels (like Nvidia's $5 Trillion / ~$206 mark). Institutional funds look at what the company is fundamentally worth at these huge round numbers rather than just chart lines.
- **George Tkaczuk on Position Sizing:** When adding to a winner on a pullback, calculate your new average cost *before* buying. If the stock pulls back normally but hits your new, higher average cost and forces you to sell, you sized up incorrectly.
- **Dave Saito-Chung on Buying Breakouts:** In a market where the Nasdaq is under its 50-day line and distribution is heavy, buying breakouts (like ANET or DDOG) is "especially perilous" and should be largely avoided.
- **Justin Nielsen on Being Cash Heavy:** "I'm not out there trying to anticipate a move that might or might not come. If that means I'm not participating in a day or two, I'm okay with that if it means protecting myself."
