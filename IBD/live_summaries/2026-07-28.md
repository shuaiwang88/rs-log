## 6. Trading Education — David Ryan's Shorting Masterclass

### The Complete Shorting System

**1. The Setup — What to Short:**
- Stocks that had huge prior runs (climactic vertical moves)
- Have suffered severe drops (40-50%+ from highs)
- Have broken their moving averages (50-day, 21-day)
- Are now attempting relief rallies

**2. Short Entries — When to Pull the Trigger:**
"Any time you get a 2-day rally, a 3-day rally back into any declining moving average in the chip stocks, in the AI stocks — you sell if you're still long. If you want to short, that's the spot where you short." The 50-day and 21-day MAs are the primary resistance zones.

**3. The NBIS Textbook Example:**
NBIS dropped from $300 to $260, then bounced for 4 days directly into the declining 50-day MA. Ryan shorted at that rejection. The stock dropped another 20-25% in 3 days. "The setup was right there — you couldn't ask for a cleaner signal."

**4. The O'Neil Rule — Timing Considerations:**
Historically, Bill O'Neil's system required waiting 3-6 months after a leader definitively topped before initiating shorts. But in modern fast-moving markets, broken high-flyers down 50%+ offer short setups sooner — "they're not coming back quickly."

**5. The Bottom-Fishing Warning:**
"Once you get a stock down 50%, they're not coming back. It usually takes months, if not years, for these things to recover. Don't be playing the bottom game." MaxLinear (MXL) at $128→$58 is not a bargain — it's a broken chart that will take extensive time to repair, if it ever does.

**6. The New Sell Regime:**
Sanmina (SANM) beat EPS, beat revenue, and raised FY26 guidance above consensus — and dropped 17%. "The market has changed its character. Beat-and-raise used to guarantee a pop. Now it's a sell signal." When the market stops rewarding good news, it's telling you something fundamental has shifted.

**7. The 6-Month Forward Rule:**
"The market looks 6 months out. It looks at what are those earnings going to be six months from now? Not what's being reported right now." Current strong earnings are backward-looking. The market is pricing in deterioration.
