## 6. Trading Education & Guest Advice

### Mike Webster — Market Navigation & Historical Precedent

**Positive Divergence Hunting (Your Mission Right Now):**
"Your mission right now is to look for things that, when the market was going lower, they held up." The model: Charles Schwab (SCHW) in October 1998. Schwab bottomed at $6.17 with the handle low at $7, while the NASDAQ made a lower low — a clear positive divergence. Schwab broke out BEFORE the follow-through day. "Bill would do that — sometimes stocks can and should be bought before a FTD if you see something really special."

**1998 Long-Term Capital Management Parallel:**
- LTCM blew up in September 1998, but the market didn't turn until it realized the Fed would "do whatever they needed to do"
- The FTD came on October 14, 1998 — Schwab and AOL had already broken out
- "In spirit, very broad strokes — the computer wouldn't pick up this precedent, but..."
- Current setup: Day 2 of rally attempt, potential double-bottom forming (or three waves down)
- "You could be on your first day off the ultimate bottom, or you could be on that gap up" — be open-minded

**Webster's Trend Change Checklist:**
1. Get above the 21-day EMA
2. Close above the 21-day
3. Low above the 21-day and close up on the day
4. Three days with the low above the 21-day, closing up

"Having a combo of FTD plus the checklist would be great. The FTD is just one signal — multiple confirmations are better."

**AMZN as Market Bellwether:**
"This is like my hope that this one ends up working out and helping to pull the market up versus being pulled up by Micron and SanDisk going back up." Amazon breaking through the 258.08 pivot with real earnings power is different from beaten-down AI names bouncing. "If it stays above 258.08, it's doing well. If not, we get another failed breakout — more market feedback."

**Methodical, Not Shotgun:**
"It's not a time for me to do a shotgun right now because of the choppiness we've had. I'm trying to find a Schwab or an AOL, so to speak, and go from there." Be selective — one good pitch at a time.

**Open-Mindedness:**
"Don't have a bullish bias or a bearish bias. Just look at reality. Yesterday was pretty important, but it could just be a one-hit wonder, a one-day wonder that happens in bear markets."

## 8. Historical Parallels & Notable Quotes

### Historical Parallel

**October 14, 1998 — Long-Term Capital Management & the FTD:**
Webster drew extended parallels between the current market and the fall of 1998. LTCM blew up in September, causing violent, inexplicable selling. The market chopped relentlessly — groups looked good for 2-3 days then got killed. The turn came when the market realized the Fed would do whatever it took. Schwab and AOL showed positive divergence (holding higher lows while NASDAQ made lower lows) and broke out BEFORE the follow-through day on October 14. That FTD kicked off the monster 1999-2000 rally. Webster: "In spirit, very broad strokes — it doesn't fit if you dissect it, the computer wouldn't pick it up. But as a precedent in general spirit, it's worth studying."
