## 1. Market Pulse

**Thursday, July 2 — Jobs miss, TSLA blowout, air pockets persist.** The June jobs report delivered a major disappointment: **+57K vs +114K expected** — half the forecast. But the market's reaction was muted, with focus shifting to Tesla's blockbuster delivery numbers: **480,000 deliveries** for Q2, smashing estimates. 

**Panel:** Ken Shreve, Justin Nielsen, Ed Carson, Scott St. Clair.

**The air pockets:** Despite TSLA's blowout, individual AI and growth names were still experiencing sudden, violent drops. "These aren't normal pullbacks — they're air pockets" (Scott St. Clair). The correction that began in late June was deepening.

**TSLA paradox:** Record deliveries but the stock struggled to hold gains — "the good news is priced in" was the consensus. With Robotaxi and Optimus timelines uncertain, deliveries alone weren't enough to sustain a rally.
