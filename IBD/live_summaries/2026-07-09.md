## 6. Trading Education

**Justin Nielsen — The Sideways Market Trap:** "This back and forth feels like October through February. It's not strong enough to pull itself out, but not weak enough to stay away completely. It does just enough to bring you back in. A lot of breakout failures, a lot of bounces that don't work. It's hard to feel like I have much of an edge."

**Ed Carson — Why Uptrends Matter:** "The reason we like uptrends is because the market trends up. If you buy on strength in a choppy market, really good chance you'll see it roll over next."

**Arnie Gutierrez — Don't Force It:** "If you don't feel comfortable in this market, don't force yourself to be trading. Protect your mental capital. Be disciplined with exits and patient with entries."

**Chris Gessel — Hedging with Leveraged Inverse ETFs:** "Switched from MUD to MUZ (2x inverse Micron). The spreads aren't wide — they're pretty tight. Makes it a whole lot easier to hedge Micron and the rest of the portfolio."

**Mike Webster — The "Imagine the Bar" Game:** "If it's at the low, what would it look like at the high? If it's at the high, what would it look like at the low? It's a game I always play to know how I would treat it."
