## 6. Trading Education — Mark Minervini Full Session

### The AccuStaff 400% Trade — Moving Average Rule
"In the 90s, I made over 400% in AccuStaff by strictly using a close below the 50-day MA as my sell rule. Leaders hold their 50-day or 10-week line. When they break it decisively, that's your exit." Webster concurred: "When Chuck and I finished the model book, we both said — why don't we just use the 10-week line? A break and then a decisive close below it. That's gold."

### The Angle Warning
Minervini defined the "angle warning": a stock in a base above a 50-day MA, but the 50-day itself is sloping downward. "The intermediate trend has not turned. The right side needs more time." Applied to SCCO: RS 77, 50-day pointing down, needs weeks/months.

### Discipline Over Results
"If you go across a street with traffic, close your eyes and cover your ears, and make it to the other side — that doesn't mean you did a smart thing. The result doesn't justify the means in the market." Winning with bad habits doesn't validate the strategy.

### Style Drift Warning
"You need to spend your time mastering something and becoming a specialist. That's what will pay you well. Style drift to me is not even a consideration." Mastery of one proven rule set (like CANSLIM) is vital — don't chase daily noise.

### Froth Signals
Retail calls about hyperextended names ("buy more if it goes down," "should triple") are warning signs. "If sentiment gets too frothy, I would sell right into it."

### Dull Markets Produce Winners
"Dull markets can produce massive breakout winners. Don't let general market indicators override individual stock setups."

### Scott St. Clair — The Magic Show
"The indexes are a magic show. They got the ball in one hand, and you're looking over here. They will do everything they can to fool you. I really think the market's weaker than it looks."

### Justin Nielsen — News Failure Events
"Jason Shapiro calls it a news failure event — the market does the opposite of what you expect. I feel like we've seen a lot of that in this cycle."
