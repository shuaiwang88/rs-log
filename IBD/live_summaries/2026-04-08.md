## 2. Mike Webster's FTD Analysis

**Closing Range is Critical:** "If we close way down in the bar, it's going to just not look like a healthy follow-through day. But it will still be a follow-through day. Not all follow-through days are created equal. I put a lot of weight on the look and feel."

**Two Key Levels:**
1. **21-day moving average** — "We're well above it. That's a positive."
2. **200-day moving average** — "Bad things tend to happen underneath the 200-day. Good things tend to happen above it."

**50-Day Resistance:** "Getting into resistance at a declining 50-day. Charles Harris — when you come up to a 50-day that's in decline, he really would sell into that. Market school: we don't count getting above a declining 50-day as a buy signal. It has to be in an incline."

**Normal vs. Abnormal:** "This is normal and natural. What excessive selling — we don't see that yet. If we were already down to the 200-day, that would not be normal and natural, and I'd be backing away."

**News Mechanics:** "The first minute to half hour — it's really computers fighting computers. Then, as people interpret the news, that's where you see the real reaction."

## 6. Trading Education — Mike Webster Masterclass

**The Thursday Tell:** "What happened on Thursday — that big upside reversal. The market gapped down and then firmed up and built throughout the day. I was caught off guard. I was like, 'Wow, this market is a lot stronger than I thought.'"

**Sunday Night Buying:** "The market on Sunday should have been down 3-4% on those aggressive tweets. It was only down a little bit. When I saw that, I'm like, 'This is a gift of all gifts.' I started buying — which is not part of my normal thing."

**Cuban Missile Crisis Comparison:** "Go back and look at the Cuban Missile Crisis from 1962. When that lifts, sometimes it happens real quick. Other events have long overhangs — after 9/11, that took years. For years after, any terrorist headline would cause a gyration."

**Computers vs. Humans:** "First: text-reading algos read the news and act. Then: momentum algos either fade (mean reversion) or go with (momentum). First 30 minutes is computers fighting computers. Then humans interpret."

**Closing Range Tool:** "Do the Fib retracement from today's high to yesterday's close. Look at which stocks are holding up best. Wait until they get back above the 50% retracement — I don't like buying in the dirt."

**The FTD Quality Test:** "Not all follow-through days are created equal. Closing range matters. Versus the 21-day matters. The look and feel matters."
