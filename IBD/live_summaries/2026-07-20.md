## 1. Market Pulse

**Monday, July 20 — Range-bound frustration continues.** The market opened the week in a choppy, sideways pattern. NASDAQ was bouncing within a range but unable to sustain any momentum above the 50-day moving average. S&P 500 held slightly better, benefiting from defensive rotation. Dow Jones and Russell 2000 were relative outperformance stories — the "value over growth" theme firmly in place.

**Guest: Scott St. Clair** — US Investing Championship competitor, swing trader. Scott delivered a blunt assessment of the psychological difficulty of this market: "It's been a very strange year for me, very, very difficult psychologically."

**Panel:** Ed Carson, Justin Nielsen, and Scott St. Clair (guest).

**Core themes:**
- Market stuck in a range — not trending up, not breaking down decisively
- Individual names experiencing "trapdoors and air pockets" — brutal selloffs on any weakness
- Rotation continues: money flowing from AI/growth into defensives, value, financials
- "Quick to take losses" — Scott's mantra for this environment
- Gap-up Mondays as exit opportunities — Mike Webster's advice: "Take the gift to reduce"

## 6. Trading Education

**Scott St. Clair — Psychological Honesty:** "In hindsight, I've had a lousy year when I look at what could have been — the SANDisks, the MUs. But if you told me on January 1 I'd be up X amount in July, I'd say OK, I'll take that. Very difficult psychologically."

**Scott St. Clair — The USIC Reporting Bias:** "In the US Investing Championship, very few participants are reporting their results. The message is that you only report if you've done well. If you're up 84%, then you report. If you're down 8% or up 2%, what's the point? The market's been a lot more difficult than it seems."

**Scott St. Clair — Manager Incentives:** "Apple's having a good day. Why? It's a great place to hide. As a money manager, you won't get fired owning Apple. You could get fired if you own FuelCell and Ouster and IREN and they all go down 50% off the highs."

**Scott St. Clair — Short Strategy:** "Quietly become more short. Around 50-50 long and short. That wasn't the case before the market opened. I shorted Meta, MU, Tesla very reluctantly, and the leveraged ETFs SOXL and KORU. They're hyperbolic, tear-your-face-off trades, but when they work, they work tremendously because they go down a lot with the leverage."

**Ed Carson — Tesla's Valuation Reality:** "Analysts give the overwhelming majority of the price target to things that aren't actually happening yet. If this were a traditional automaker with a PE of 10, these numbers would have driven things up a lot. But it has an enormous PE based on things it isn't doing."

**Scott St. Clair — BYD Anecdote:** "In Italy, my rental car was a BYD. Beautiful car, ran great, probably half the price of a Tesla. The product looks awesome. In Europe, they have a lot more than we have. I don't know if it's illegal to buy one here, but the tariff is 100% or something."
