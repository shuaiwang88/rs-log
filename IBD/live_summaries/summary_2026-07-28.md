# IBD Live Summary — 2026-07-28

*Source: IBD Live transcript_2026-07-28_09.53.23.txt*

## 1. Market Pulse
The market is characterized as "two different markets." Value stocks are thriving, with the S&P Value ETF (IVE) and the Equal Weight S&P (RSP) hitting new highs. Conversely, growth is getting crushed. The S&P Growth ETF (IVW) and the QQQ are making new lows and have broken decisively below their 10-day, 21-day, and 50-day moving averages. Semiconductors and AI hardware stocks are in confirmed downtrends. The panel emphasizes that this is not an environment for buying growth on the dip, but rather an environment for cash, value sectors, or shorting broken growth names on relief rallies.

## 3. Leaderboard & SwingTrade Updates
The team is heavily in cash regarding growth stocks, with David Ryan pointing out that the IBD invested position (0-20%) is appropriate for growth investors, but perhaps too low if you are invested in the leading value sectors like healthcare, refiners, and financials. Chris Gessel and Harold Morris mentioned stepping away entirely rather than forcing trades, noting that "cash is a position."

## 6. Trading Education & Guest Advice
- **David Ryan on "Change in Character":** Stocks like Sanmina (SANM) are beating earnings and raising guidance, but the stock is selling off 17%. When good news is met with heavy selling, the market character has changed to negative.
- **David Ryan on Shorting:** In a broken growth market, look for former high-flying leaders that have broken their 50-day lines. When they experience 2 to 4-day relief rallies *up into* declining 21-day or 50-day moving averages (like NBIS and CRDO), those are prime low-risk short entries.
- **David Ryan on Bottom Fishing:** Avoid stocks like MXL that have fallen 50% or more. They rarely V-bottom. They will require months or years of base building before they are ready for a new uptrend. 
- **Ed Carson on Survival:** "There's always some of the heroes that do not make it... some of the soldiers don't take that hill." Even if a theme (like AI) is real, not every stock will survive the shakeout.
