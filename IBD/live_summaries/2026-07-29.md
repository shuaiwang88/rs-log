## 6. Trading Education — Mark Minervini's Full Masterclass

### Risk Management Above All (43 Years of Practice)
"The secret to multi-decade success — like Bill O'Neil's, David Ryan's, and my career — is not getting into deep losses in the first place. When you make a mistake, cut ties immediately." Minervini's core philosophy: survival first, returns second. Hope is the enemy: "Hope is a f***** — hoping a losing trade turns around is the fastest way to blow up."

### The 6-Year Preparation Rule
Minervini spent 6 years preparing before his breakout success in 1990. "You need to go through multiple market cycles to develop true pattern recognition. There are no shortcuts. Become a specialist in one proven style — don't jump between strategies."

### IPO Base Strategy (Livermore's 1920s Playbook)
Drawing from Jesse Livermore's *How to Trade in Stocks* (1920s): buy recent IPOs (from the last several years) when they build a base near or just above all-time highs and break out into new highs within an established uptrend. Application: YOU (Clear Secure) — recent IPO approaching all-time highs in the high 50s/60s, earnings turned from negative to positive. "It needs to build a tight right-side base. I'm using forward imagination — looking weeks out, not days."

### Position Sizing & Earnings Risk (PAX Case Study)
"Never go into earnings with a large position without a pre-established cushion." The PAX playbook:
1. Bought June 24 coming out of a base
2. Up 20%+ → trim 15-25% into strength
3. The trim finances a break-even stop on the remainder
4. For the remaining position, double the implied volatility to size — if the stock could move 8%, size so that an 8% loss is acceptable
5. "You're playing with the house's money through earnings"

### The 70% Rule (Where to Hunt)
O'Neil's historical study of the biggest winners: 70% came from just 4-5 areas. Retail/Consumer, Technology, Leisure/Entertainment, and Drugs/Medical/Biotech. "Deep cyclicals like oil, steel, and paper lack multi-year secular earnings growth and get demerits. Focus your time in the top groups."

### Bottom-Fishing Warning (SNDK Example)
"I would never be buying here. I don't care if it bounces back and I miss it, and I don't even care if I missed a low." On SanDisk down massively from highs: "The snapback rally is the gift — to lock in profits and protect accumulated gains, not a new entry." Minervini doesn't buy broken leaders. "You're not paid to be a hero catching falling knives."

### Market Bifurcation & Adaptation
"I've been trading for 43 years, and this is probably the most I've seen the market split." In this environment: be hedged (short S&P), hold select longs with profit cushions, avoid the broken areas entirely. "Don't try to be long everything or short everything — be precise."

### Options Warning
"Options success rates are exceptionally low." The triple hurdle: direction, magnitude, AND time horizon must all be right. "High volatility premiums work against you. The vast majority of options buyers lose money over time."

### Historical Charts Haven't Changed Since the 1800s
Charts of Coca-Cola in the 1930s look identical to Monster Beverage decades later. "Human psychology doesn't change. What HAS changed is the speed of information and execution. The patterns remain."

### Oil & Geopolitics
"We go from making up with Iran one day to bombing the f*** out of them the next day." Minervini on energy trading: "Stay clear or trade them lightly. The volatility and headline unpredictability make them unsuitable for my style."
